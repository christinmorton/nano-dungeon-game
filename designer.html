Lighting
Torchlit — tight sight, harsh memory: the underground feel, and no sky. Daylight — under open sky, so the floor follows the day/night cycle. Set by the dungeon type; change it here to override.
Time of day
Only affects floors with a sky. On Cycle the hour advances with the turn counter and keeps running while you're indoors — at night your sight shrinks, a torch finally matters, and enemies see less too.

Dungeon
Dungeon type
The whole dungeon's look, in one pick. A type sets the ground and wall terrain together, and both are auto-tiled — edges and corners are chosen per cell, so you never pick tiles yourself. Applies to every floor.
Global enemies
Enemy source for the whole dungeon. Authored — rooms only ever use their own mix or this pool (empty pool = empty room, nothing random). Random — legacy: authored mixes still win, then rooms fall back to random spawns.
Enemy behavior
Suppress enemy categories across the whole dungeon. Off = normal generation.
The dungeon-wide enemy pool — a default any room can draw from. Presets are named mixes shared across this pool and the per-room mixes.
Enemy perception
How far and how wide enemies can see. 1 = the default; lower = tighter, so enemies notice you later. Sight range scales the sight distance (cones and circles); Cone width scales the forward arc.
